APPLICATION MATRIX

Food processing fasteners are engineered for hygienic, corrosion-resistant, easy-to-clean, and high-reliability equipment where sanitation, food safety, washdown durability, chemical resistance, thermal stability, vibration control, and traceability are essential. From mixers, blenders, conveyors, cutting systems, filling machines, storage tanks, cooking equipment, hoppers, pumps, valves, weighing systems, inspection lines, robotic handling systems, packaging machines, dairy lines, beverage plants, and cold-chain equipment, every fastening solution must support clean operation, safe production, and long lifecycle performance.


Pradako supports food processing, beverage, dairy, bakery, packaging, pharmaceutical-adjacent, and hygienic automation applications with fastening solutions designed for stainless steel assemblies, smooth finishes, corrosion protection, cleanability, secure locking, quick maintenance, and traceable quality.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Food Processing & Packaging Fasteners

Food processing and packaging applications require fastening solutions that support hygienic design, food safety, corrosion resistance, cleanability, contamination control, washdown performance, regulatory compliance, and full traceability.

01

Hygienic Design & Food Safety

Food processing equipment requires smooth, cleanable, crevice-minimized fastening solutions that support sanitation, product purity, and food-contact safety expectations.

02

Corrosion & Chemical Resistance

Frequent cleaning with water, steam, caustic solutions, acids, disinfectants, and detergents demands stainless steel, passivated surfaces, electropolished finishes, PTFE-compatible hardware, and food-grade sealing materials.

03

Clean-In-Place & Maintenance Efficiency

CIP and COP systems benefit from sanitary clamps, quick-release fasteners, captive screws, and tool-friendly hardware that support rapid cleaning, inspection, and changeover.

04

Contamination Control

Food equipment fasteners should support smooth finishes, secure retention, clean geometry, and traceability to reduce loose-part risk and maintain safe processing environments.

05

Thermal & Mechanical Stability

Cooking, baking, freezing, blending, cutting, and packaging systems experience heat, cold, vibration, torque, and cyclic movement. Correct materials and locking methods support stable operation.

06

Washdown & IP-Rated Equipment Support

Weighing systems, sensors, control panels, inspection machines, and automated lines require fasteners that support sealed enclosures, hygienic wiring, moisture protection, and reliable washdown performance.

07

Standards & Project Alignment

Food processing applications may follow FDA, 3-A Sanitary Standards, EHEDG, GMP, HACCP, NSF, ISO, CE, NEMA, IP, and customer-specific hygiene requirements depending on equipment type and export market.

08

Traceability & Documentation

Food equipment manufacturers benefit from material test certificates, stainless steel grade traceability, surface finish records, coating reports, dimensional inspection reports, and batch-level documentation.
