Rugged Fastening Solutions for Heavy-Duty Equipment

APPLICATION MATRIX

Heavy machinery and equipment fasteners are engineered for high-load, high-vibration, high-impact environments where strength, fatigue resistance, torque retention, wear protection, corrosion resistance, and field serviceability are essential. From chassis frames, undercarriages, powertrains, gearboxes, hydraulic systems, buckets, blades, track systems, operator cabins, axles, brakes, steering systems, cranes, booms, and maintenance assemblies, every fastening solution must support rugged performance under demanding industrial, construction, mining, earthmoving, agricultural, and material-handling conditions.


Pradako supports heavy equipment manufacturers and maintenance networks with high-strength fastening solutions designed for structural durability, vibration control, shock resistance, abrasion protection, secure locking, protective coatings, and dependable lifecycle performance in the toughest operating environments.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Industrial Fasteners

Industrial fasteners support machinery, process equipment, production systems, automation infrastructure, maintenance operations, and heavy-duty industrial environments where reliability, precision, safety, corrosion resistance, and long-term performance are critical.

01

Wide Industrial Coverage

Industrial fasteners support painting equipment, coating lines, chemical plants, machine tools, compressors, hydraulic systems, ovens, conveyors, skids, tanks, textile machinery, printing equipment, rubber machinery, plastic machinery, and general plant equipment.

02

Vibration & Torque Retention

Industrial machinery operates under motors, pumps, rollers, compressors, gearboxes, conveyors, and cyclic movement. Reliable fasteners help maintain alignment, reduce loosening, and support stable equipment performance.

03

Corrosion & Chemical Resistance

Painting systems, plating lines, pretreatment tanks, filtration systems, washdown areas, and chemical process equipment require stainless steel, zinc plating, hot-dip galvanizing, zinc flake, PTFE coating, and application-specific finishes.

04

Thermal Stability

Powder coating lines, curing ovens, industrial dryers, furnaces, heaters, moulding machines, and process equipment require fasteners capable of handling heat cycles, expansion, and long-duty operation.

05

Precision & Alignment

Machine tools, printing equipment, textile machinery, automation panels, and conveyor systems depend on fasteners that support accurate positioning, smooth adjustment, repeatable assembly, and consistent machine performance.

06

Maintenance & Serviceability

Industrial equipment must remain easy to inspect, open, repair, clean, and reassemble. Captive screws, quick-release fasteners, standardized bolts, washers, pins, and replacement kits help reduce downtime.

07

Safety & Plant Reliability

Machine guards, platforms, tanks, skids, pressure lines, electrical cabinets, and moving assemblies require fasteners that support operator safety, equipment stability, and long-term plant reliability.

08

Traceability & Documentation

Industrial projects benefit from material certificates, coating thickness reports, dimensional inspection records, hardness reports, thread checks, packing labels, and batch-level traceability.
