Pradako supports petrochemical plants, refineries and process infrastructure with engineered fastening solutions designed for flanged joints, high-pressure piping, equipment assemblies, structural connections, maintenance systems and critical process applications requiring strength, durability, controlled quality and consistent performance.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Additional Petrochemical Considerations

Petrochemical facilities require fasteners capable of handling pressure, temperature variation, chemical exposure, vibration, corrosion, and critical process reliability while maintaining long-term performance, safety, traceability, and maintenance efficiency.

01

Pressure & Leak Integrity

Petrochemical plants rely on secure bolted joints across flanges, vessels, valves, pumps, reactors and piping systems. Fasteners must support controlled tightening, gasket compression, clamp load retention and long-term sealing performance.

02

Corrosion Resistance

Petrochemical environments may involve moisture, chemicals, hydrocarbons, salt air, industrial fumes and outdoor exposure. Stainless steel, coated steel, galvanized finishes, nickel finishes, zinc flake coatings and application-specific surface treatments help support longer service life.

03

Heat & Thermal Cycling

Process plants include high-temperature piping, fired heaters, boilers, heat exchangers and reactors. Fasteners used in these areas must support thermal expansion, temperature variation, oxidation resistance and stable mechanical performance.

04

Vibration & Fatigue Resistance

Pumps, compressors, rotating equipment, skids and process lines are exposed to vibration, dynamic loads and pressure cycles. High-strength fasteners, controlled torque and suitable locking arrangements help maintain stable assemblies.

05

Material Selection

Material selection depends on temperature, pressure, chemical exposure, corrosion environment, connected materials and service requirements. Common options include ASTM A193 B7/B7M, B8/B8M, A320 L7/L7M, A194 2H/2HM/8/8M, stainless steel, alloy steel, duplex stainless steel and coated carbon steel.

06

Traceability & Documentation

Petrochemical projects often require strong documentation, including material test certificates, heat treatment records, coating reports, dimensional inspection, mechanical testing, process control records and batch-level traceability.

07

Maintenance & Shutdown Efficiency

Fasteners used in petrochemical plants should support planned maintenance, shutdown work, inspection access and replacement efficiency. Consistent dimensions, controlled threading and suitable surface protection help improve service performance.

08

Controlled Quality

Thread accuracy, surface finish, coating control, mechanical strength, hardness, dimensional accuracy and tightening performance are important for long-term reliability across petrochemical process systems.
