APPLICATION MATRIX

Mining and tunneling fasteners are engineered for extreme underground and surface operations where high impact, abrasion, vibration, ground pressure, moisture, corrosion, heavy loads, dust exposure, and safety-critical reliability are essential. From tunnel boring machines, rock bolts, rail systems, conveyors, ventilation ducts, crushers, pumps, dewatering systems, elevators, shotcrete linings, ground support systems, and underground service infrastructure, every fastening solution must support strength, safety, wear resistance, corrosion protection, and dependable lifecycle performance.


Pradako supports mining, tunneling, excavation, underground construction, mineral processing, and heavy-duty infrastructure applications with fastening solutions designed for rugged environments, secure locking, high tensile strength, fatigue resistance, abrasion protection, protective coatings, and traceable quality.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Mining & Tunneling Fasteners

Mining and tunneling applications require fastening solutions that support high-impact loads, structural stability, wear resistance, corrosion protection, vibration control, safety systems, maintenance efficiency, and complete lifecycle traceability.

01

High Impact, Load & Fatigue Resistance

Mining and tunneling equipment works under heavy cutting forces, crushing loads, shock impact, vibration, and continuous cyclic stress. High-tensile and heat-treated fasteners support rugged performance in demanding underground and surface operations.

02

Ground Support & Structural Safety

Rock bolts, resin bolts, cable bolts, split sets, mesh clips, rebar couplers, and tunnel lining fasteners support roof control, wall stability, slope reinforcement, and safe underground working conditions.

03

Abrasion & Wear Resistance

TBM cutterheads, crushers, grinding mills, conveyors, buckets, drilling tools, and excavation attachments operate against rock, ore, dust, and abrasive materials. Hardened materials and protective coatings help extend component life.

04

Corrosion Protection in Harsh Environments

Moisture, groundwater, acidic drainage, humidity, dust, and chemical exposure demand corrosion-resistant fasteners. Galvanizing, zinc flake, epoxy coatings, phosphate finishes, stainless steel, and specialty coatings support long service life.

05

Vibration, Torque Retention & Secure Locking

Conveyors, crushers, pumps, fans, rail systems, drilling rigs, and mining vehicles experience continuous vibration. Lock nuts, locking washers, thread-locking systems, controlled preload, and secure clamping support stable assemblies.

06

Ventilation, Fire Safety & Environmental Support

Fasteners used in ventilation ducts, fan systems, dust suppression, fire-safety equipment, and monitoring systems must support safe airflow, secure mounting, rapid access, and dependable underground operation.

07

Maintenance & Downtime Reduction

Mining and tunneling operations value fast replacement of wear parts, liners, conveyor components, pumps, guards, and service panels. Service-friendly fasteners help improve uptime and reduce maintenance delays.

08

Traceability & Documentation

Mining and tunneling projects benefit from material test certificates, heat treatment records, coating reports, dimensional inspection reports, proof-load testing, torque records, and batch-level traceability.
