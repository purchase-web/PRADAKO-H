APPLICATION MATRIX

Aerospace fasteners are engineered for safety-critical assemblies where strength, weight reduction, vibration resistance, fatigue life, corrosion protection, and traceability are essential. From airframe structures and wing assemblies to landing gear, engines, interiors, avionics, fuel systems, and rotor assemblies, every fastening solution must support precision, reliability, and long-term performance under demanding flight conditions.


Pradako supports aerospace and defence applications with engineered fastening solutions designed for structural integrity, lightweight construction, corrosion resistance, secure locking, and consistent quality across critical assemblies.

DESIGN & COMPLIANCE

Additional Aerospace Considerations

Aerospace fastening systems must satisfy stringent aviation standards while maintaining structural integrity, lightweight performance, corrosion resistance, traceability, and long-term operational reliability throughout the aircraft lifecycle.

01

Regulatory & Customer Compliance

Aerospace fasteners are expected to align with strict aviation, defence, and customer-specific requirements, including material certification, process control, dimensional accuracy, and complete traceability.

02

Material Selection

Weight reduction is a key priority in aircraft design. Titanium, aluminium alloys, stainless steel, nickel alloys, and high-strength steels are selected to balance strength, fatigue life, corrosion resistance, and weight efficiency.

03

Corrosion Resistance

Aircraft operate in demanding environments including moisture, salt air, fuel exposure, temperature variation, and pressure cycles. Protective coatings, passivation, sealing systems, and corrosion-resistant alloys support long service life.

04

Vibration & Fatigue Resistance

Continuous vibration, aerodynamic loads, engine forces, landing impacts, and pressurization cycles require secure locking systems, high fatigue resistance, controlled torque, and reliable clamp-load retention.

05

Traceability & Documentation

Aerospace supply chains require comprehensive documentation including material test certificates, process records, inspection reports, coating records, heat treatment records, dimensional reports, and batch-level traceability.

06

Safety & Maintenance

Aerospace fasteners support easy inspection, replacement, and maintenance. Captive fasteners, quick-release systems, locking hardware, and corrosion-resistant products improve service efficiency and operational safety.
