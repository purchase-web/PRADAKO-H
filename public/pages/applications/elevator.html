APPLICATION MATRIX

Elevator and escalator fasteners are engineered for safety-critical moving systems where load capacity, vibration control, alignment accuracy, passenger safety, corrosion resistance, service access, and long lifecycle reliability are essential. From elevator car frames, guide rails, brackets, door mechanisms, safety gears, escalator steps, handrails, motor mounts, control cabinets, shaft anchors, inspection panels, and modernization systems, every fastening solution must support smooth operation, secure assembly, and dependable performance under repeated daily cycles.


Pradako supports vertical transportation applications with fastening solutions designed for structural stability, controlled torque, vibration resistance, precise alignment, safety-system reliability, protective coatings, and traceable quality across elevators, escalators, moving walkways, and passenger mobility infrastructure.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Elevators & Escalators Fasteners

Elevator and escalator fastening systems must support passenger safety, controlled movement, vibration resistance, regulatory compliance, corrosion protection, serviceability, and long-term operational reliability.

01

Safety-Critical Load Performance

Elevators and escalators operate under continuous passenger movement, repeated start-stop cycles, static loads, dynamic loads, and emergency load conditions. Fasteners must support secure assembly, controlled torque, and dependable structural performance.

02

Alignment Accuracy & Smooth Operation

Guide rails, brackets, door tracks, escalator steps, comb plates, and drive systems require precise fastening to maintain smooth travel, reduced vibration, accurate movement, and passenger comfort.

03

Vibration, Fatigue & Torque Retention

Motors, gearboxes, brakes, rollers, step assemblies, doors, and safety systems experience repeated motion. Locking hardware, proper preload, and fatigue-resistant materials support long-term reliability.

04

Regulatory & Safety Alignment

Vertical transportation systems may follow ASME A17.1 / CSA B44, EN 81, EN 115, ISO 8100, IEC, NEMA, local lift regulations, fire-safety requirements, and customer-specific inspection standards depending on region and application.

05

Corrosion Resistance & Surface Protection

Indoor installations, outdoor elevators, public transit hubs, parking structures, coastal locations, and humid environments require suitable coatings such as zinc plating, hot-dip galvanizing, phosphate coatings, stainless steel, black oxide, and zinc flake.

06

Passenger Interaction & Aesthetics

Visible fasteners used on handrails, cabin panels, doors, balustrades, and interior trims require smooth edges, clean finish, corrosion resistance, and a premium appearance.

07

Maintenance & Lifecycle Support

Captive screws, quick-release pins, inspection-access fasteners, and service-friendly hardware allow faster maintenance, safe inspection, easier replacement, and reliable long term operation.

08

Traceability & Documentation

Elevator and escalator systems benefit from material test certificates, coating records, dimensional inspection reports, torque control records, batch traceability, and customer-specific quality documentation.
