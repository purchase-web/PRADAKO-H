APPLICATION MATRIX

Pipeline fasteners are engineered for high-pressure, high-stress, and safety-critical pipeline systems where sealing integrity, corrosion protection, vibration resistance, temperature stability, sour service suitability, controlled bolting, and long-term reliability are essential. From mainline pipe joints, flanges, connectors, valve stations, pump and compressor stations, metering skids, pig launchers, pipeline supports, branch connections, subsea pipelines, and emergency shutdown systems, every fastening solution must support secure assembly, pressure containment, inspection readiness, and dependable lifecycle performance.


Pradako supports oil, gas, water, process, offshore, subsea, refinery, petrochemical, and energy pipeline applications with fastening solutions designed for high-load joints, pressure-rated assemblies, corrosion-resistant coatings, secure sealing, traceability, and long service life.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Pipeline Fasteners

Pipeline systems require fastening solutions that support pressure containment, sealing integrity, corrosion resistance, thermal movement, vibration control, maintenance access, and long-term operational reliability across onshore, offshore, transmission, and distribution infrastructure.

01

High-Pressure Sealing Integrity

Pipeline flanges, connectors, valves, pig traps, regulators, and repair clamps require controlled clamp load, proper gasket compression, and reliable bolted-joint performance for safe pressure containment.

02

Regulatory & Code Alignment

Pipeline applications may follow API, ASME B31.3, ASME B31.4, ASME B31.8, ASME Section VIII, ISO, NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156, DNV, IECEx, ATEX, DOT, PNGRB, and customer-specific project requirements depending on pipeline type, location, and service condition.

03

Corrosion & Sour Service Suitability

Pipelines face soil exposure, moisture, seawater, chemical media, hydrocarbons, and H₂S sour service conditions. Correct material selection and coatings such as hot-dip galvanizing, zinc flake, epoxy, PTFE / fluoropolymer, stainless steel, and specialty alloys support long-term durability.

04

Vibration, Pulsation & Fatigue Resistance

Pump stations, compressor stations, valve assemblies, branch connections, supports, and subsea pipelines experience pulsation, surge loads, vibration, and cyclic stress. Secure locking, proper preload, and fatigue-resistant materials support connection stability.

05

Thermal Expansion & Movement Control

Pipeline supports, anchors, clamps, hangers, and branch connections must accommodate thermal expansion, contraction, settlement, soil movement, wave movement, and operating temperature variation.

06

Inspection, Pigging & Maintenance Access

Pig launchers, receivers, valve stations, metering skids, repair clamps, and monitoring systems require fasteners that support safe access, secure closure, periodic inspection, and efficient maintenance operations.

07

Traceability & Documentation

Pipeline projects benefit from material test certificates, heat treatment records, coating reports, hardness reports, dimensional inspection reports, torque-control records, NDT documentation, and batch-level traceability.
