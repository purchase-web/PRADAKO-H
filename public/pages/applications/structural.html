KEY PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

Key Performance Requirements

Structural fastening systems must deliver reliable load transfer, controlled preload, corrosion resistance, installation efficiency, sealing performance, material holding strength, and complete traceability to support safe and long-term structural applications.

01

Structural Strength

Structural fasteners must support tension, shear, vibration, wind loads, dead loads, live loads, and dynamic forces across steel, timber, concrete, and hybrid structures.

02

Controlled Preload

EN 14399 and high-strength structural bolting applications rely on controlled tightening, correct washer selection, and stable clamp load for safe joint performance.

03

Weather & Corrosion Protection

Outdoor structures, roofing systems, PEB buildings, bridges, façades, and infrastructure require protective coatings that support long service life across rain, humidity, UV exposure, industrial air, and coastal conditions.

04

Installation Efficiency

Self-drilling screws, roofing screws, structural bolts, anchors, and pre-assembled fastener sets help improve site speed, reduce installation complexity, and support repeatable assembly quality.

05

Sealing & Building Envelope Performance

Roofing and cladding fasteners must support leak-resistant fixing through correct washer compression, proper seating, coating integrity, and compatible screw selection.

06

Timber & Board Holding Power

Wood screws and chipboard screws require accurate thread design, sharp points, clean driving, strong pull-out resistance, and stable holding in timber, MDF, particle board, and engineered panels.

07

Traceability & Quality Records

Structural projects benefit from material certificates, coating thickness reports, hardness reports, dimensional inspection reports, thread checks, torque records, and batch-level traceability.
