KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Valve Fasteners

Valve fastening applications require pressure containment, sealing integrity, controlled torque, thermal stability, corrosion resistance, vibration durability, standards compliance, and complete traceability to support safe and reliable flow-control operations across industrial environments.

01

Pressure Integrity & Sealing Reliability

Valve body, bonnet, flange, gland, and cover fasteners maintain gasket compression and support safe pressure containment across flow-control systems.

02

Controlled Bolting & Torque Retention

Valves require accurate tightening to maintain leak-resistant joints, actuator alignment, packing compression, and long-term operating stability.

03

Temperature & Thermal Cycling Resistance

Steam valves, cryogenic valves, boiler valves, LNG valves, and process valves experience thermal expansion and contraction. Correct fastener materials support stable performance.

04

Corrosion & Chemical Compatibility

Valve fasteners may operate in water, steam, chemicals, seawater, hydrocarbons, gases, and sour service conditions. Stainless steel, alloy steel, duplex grades, PTFE coatings, zinc flake, and specialty finishes support durability.

05

Vibration & Cycle Durability

Control valves, actuated valves, rotating equipment valves, marine valves, and process valves experience repeated movement and vibration. Locking systems and fatigue-resistant fasteners support stable operation.

06

Standards & Project Alignment

Valve applications may align with API 600, API 602, API 6D, API 609, API 598, ASME B16.34, ASME B31.3, ASTM, ISO, EN, NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156, PED, and customer-specific project requirements depending on service condition.

07

Traceability & Documentation

Valve projects benefit from material test certificates, heat treatment records, hardness reports, coating reports, dimensional inspection reports, torque records, and batch-level traceability.
