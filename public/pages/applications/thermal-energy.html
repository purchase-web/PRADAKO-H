APPLICATION MATRIX

Thermal power fasteners are engineered for high-temperature, high-pressure, high-vibration, and continuous-duty environments where structural strength, pressure containment, thermal stability, corrosion resistance, fatigue performance, controlled bolting, and long service life are essential. From boilers, turbines, generators, condensers, cooling towers, chimneys, pipe racks, pumps, valves, coal handling systems, ash handling systems, heat exchangers, ESP systems, FGD units, substations, and maintenance assemblies, every fastening solution must support safe power generation and dependable plant operation.


Pradako supports thermal power plants, captive power plants, utility stations, combined-cycle plants, boiler manufacturers, turbine systems, balance-of-plant equipment, and energy infrastructure with fastening solutions designed for high-load joints, high-temperature service, vibration control, corrosion protection, traceability, and long lifecycle reliability.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Thermal Power Fasteners

Thermal power plants require fastening solutions that support high-temperature performance, pressure containment, vibration resistance, corrosion protection, maintenance accessibility, and long-term operational reliability.

01

High Temperature & Pressure Performance

Boilers, steam lines, turbines, heat exchangers, valves, and pressure systems operate under demanding temperature and pressure conditions. Chrome-moly steels, B7, B16, stainless steels, and heat-resistant alloys help maintain strength and joint stability.

02

Thermal Cycling & Expansion Control

Power plants experience repeated heating and cooling cycles. Fasteners must support expansion, contraction, controlled preload, and dimensional stability across boilers, steam piping, condensers, and turbine systems.

03

Vibration & Fatigue Resistance

Turbines, generators, pumps, fans, conveyors, crushers, and rotating equipment create continuous vibration and cyclic loads. Locking systems, controlled torque, and fatigue-resistant materials support reliable plant operation.

04

Corrosion, Ash & Chemical Exposure Protection

Cooling water, steam, flue gas, ash, coal dust, humidity, acids, and outdoor exposure demand suitable coatings and materials. Stainless steel, hot-dip galvanizing, zinc flake, phosphate, epoxy, and acid-resistant coatings help extend service life.

05

Pressure Sealing & Leak Prevention

Boiler drums, manways, flanges, valves, pumps, heat exchangers, and piping systems require fastening solutions that maintain gasket compression, pressure containment, and safe process operation.

06

Environmental Control Systems

ESP, FGD, ducting, chimneys, ash handling, and emission-control systems require fasteners that support sealing, corrosion protection, chemical durability, and long-term environmental compliance.

07

Safety, Inspection & Shutdown Maintenance

Thermal plants depend on scheduled inspections, NDT, torque checks, retightening, replacement, and shutdown maintenance. Fasteners must support safe access, service efficiency, and reliable reassembly.

08

Standards & Project Alignment

Thermal power applications may follow ASME BPVC, ASME B31.1, ASME B31.3, API, ASTM, ISO, IEC, IBR, EN, DIN, NFPA, OSHA, local boiler regulations, utility specifications, and customer project standards depending on equipment type and region.

09

Traceability & Documentation

Thermal power projects benefit from material test certificates, heat treatment records, coating reports, hardness reports, dimensional inspection reports, torque records, NDT documentation, and batch-level traceability.
