KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Highlights for Automotive Fasteners

Automotive fastening systems must deliver safety, reliability, traceability, corrosion resistance, dimensional accuracy, and repeatable performance across high-volume production environments. From chassis and powertrain assemblies to braking systems, interiors, electrical modules, and commercial vehicles, fasteners play a critical role in vehicle quality and long-term durability.

01

IATF 16949 Quality Discipline

Automotive fasteners require controlled processes, documented inspection, process validation, corrective action discipline, and continuous improvement. IATF 16949 systems support consistency across OEM and Tier supply chains.

02

0 PPM Performance Mindset

Automotive supply demands defect prevention at every stage. Pradako's approach supports a 0 PPM mindset through process control, inspection discipline, traceability, sorting, documentation, and reliable batch monitoring.

03

PPAP, APQP & Documentation Readiness

Automotive programs may require PPAP, APQP, FAI, FPI, control plans, process flow diagrams, inspection reports, MSA, SPC, material certificates, coating reports, and customer-specific documentation.

04

Safety-Critical Reliability

Chassis, brakes, steering, suspension, wheels, axles, seats, and structural body parts depend on fastening solutions that support fatigue strength, clamp-load retention, vibration resistance, and long service life.

05

Torque Retention & Vibration Control

Vehicles operate through continuous vibration, road shock, braking loads, acceleration, and thermal cycles. Locking systems, correct coatings, controlled tightening, and precision thread quality support stable joints.

06

Corrosion Protection & Coating Performance

Automotive fasteners face road salt, rain, humidity, heat, chemicals, oils, and underbody exposure. Zinc plating, zinc flake, phosphate, black oxide, nickel, stainless steel, and customer-specific coatings support durability.

07

Dimensional Accuracy & Assembly Efficiency

High-volume automotive assembly requires consistent dimensions, accurate threads, clean drive features, correct head forms, reliable fitment, and smooth installation on manual, semi-automatic, and robotic assembly lines.

08

Traceability from Raw Material to Dispatch

Automotive programs benefit from heat-wise raw material traceability, batch numbers, process records, heat treatment reports, coating records, inspection data, sorting records, packing labels, and dispatch identity.

09

Testing & Validation Support

Automotive fastener quality may include tensile testing, proof load testing, hardness testing, thread gauge inspection, coating thickness measurement, salt spray testing, torque checks, visual inspection, and optical sorting.

10

Global Automotive Supply Readiness

Automotive supply chains require consistency, documentation, packaging discipline, delivery planning, customer-specific requirements, IMDS support where applicable, and long-term repeat supply capability.
