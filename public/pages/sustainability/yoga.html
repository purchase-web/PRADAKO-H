THE PROPOSAL THAT BECAME A GLOBAL MOVEMENT

A VISION ROOTED IN BALANCE, WELL-BEING AND HARMONY WITH NATURE.

In 2014, at the United Nations General Assembly, Narendra Modi, Prime Minister of India, introduced a vision that placed yoga at the intersection of human well-being, environmental awareness and collective responsibility.

That vision led to the adoption of the International Day of Yoga, first observed on 21 June 2015—uniting millions across cultures in a shared journey of mindfulness.

2015

THE FIRST INTERNATIONAL DAY OF YOGA
