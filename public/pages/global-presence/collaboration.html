Precision Is Our Product. Partnership Is Our Approach.

Engineering depth, modern production and secure collaboration under one long-term partnership.

Founded in the 1960s, Pradako Mechanical and Engineering Works has grown into a trusted fastener manufacturing partner with seven manufacturing facilities, combining decades of engineering expertise with modern production capabilities.

Our comprehensive product range includes precision screws, bolts, nuts, threaded rods, rivets and specialised fastening solutions supported by dedicated customer production lines, state-of-the-art manufacturing infrastructure and rigorous quality systems aligned with global standards such as ASTM, DIN, ISO and EN.

With a strong presence across international markets and experience supporting more than 60 industrial application sectors — including automotive, aerospace, defence, electronics and heavy engineering — we operate with the highest levels of professional integrity, strict confidentiality and responsible data protection practices.

Every collaboration is conducted under secure frameworks aligned with NDA-driven partnerships, customer-specific requirements and clearly established project responsibilities.

Guided by continuous research and development, innovation-focused manufacturing, sustainability initiatives and global regulatory compliance, we remain committed to building long-term strategic collaborations that deliver precision, reliability and sustained value for partners worldwide.
