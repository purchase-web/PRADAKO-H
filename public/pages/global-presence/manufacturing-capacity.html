Manufacturing Expansion Program

Expanding capacity for the next era
of industrial growth.

Global demand for high-quality engineered fasteners continues to rise, driven by rapid infrastructure development, manufacturing expansion, and the growing preference for reliable supply partners.

At Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works, we view this moment as an opportunity to strengthen our role as a trusted manufacturing partner for global industries. Our ongoing investments focus on expanding manufacturing capacity to support both our existing partners and the growing requirements of new customers across diverse industrial sectors.

Looking ahead, our vision is to build manufacturing capacity capable of supporting up to 40,000 metric tonnes of fasteners annually — serving both our long-standing customers and new industrial partnerships.
