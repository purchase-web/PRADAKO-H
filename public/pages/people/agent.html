THE PURPOSE OF THE PROGRAMME

The International Agents Programme has been developed to create a structured, professional and measurable presence in important industrial markets.

An appointed agent acts as a local market connection between PRADAKO’s manufacturing capabilities and customers seeking dependable international sourcing and product-development support.

The programme is intended to:

  • Establish PRADAKO in strategically relevant markets
  • Develop relationships with OEMs and industrial organisations
  • Connect with importers, distributors and sourcing companies
  • Generate technically and commercially qualified enquiries
  • Support vendor-registration and supplier-approval processes
  • Identify customised and drawing-based product opportunities
  • Strengthen local customer communication
  • Build awareness of PRADAKO’s manufacturing capabilities
  • Support exhibitions and regional business-development activities
  • Create repeat orders and long-term supply programmes
  • Develop market intelligence for future products and capacities
  • Build a credible and consistent international brand presence

The objective is to develop markets with patience, discipline and purpose.

AGENT, DISTRIBUTOR AND STOCKIST
CLEARLY DEFINED ROLES

A clear commercial structure protects both the representative and PRADAKO.

Focus area

International Sales Agent

An agent promotes PRADAKO, introduces prospective customers, generates enquiries and supports the commercial development of approved accounts.

Under the normal agency model:

  • PRADAKO supplies and invoices the customer directly
  • The customer places the order directly with PRADAKO
  • The agent supports communication and market development
  • The agent earns an agreed commission on eligible realised business
  • The agent holds no inventory unless separately agreed
  • The agent cannot bind PRADAKO without written authority
Focus area

Distributor

A distributor normally purchases products from PRADAKO and resells them within an agreed market.

A distributor may:

  • Carry local inventory
  • Manage local customer credit
  • Set resale prices subject to applicable law
  • Provide regional deliveries
  • Maintain local product availability
  • Invest in local sales and promotion
  • Operate under separate distributor terms
Focus area

Authorised Stockist

A stockist may maintain agreed standard products for faster regional supply while operating within an approved product, territory and brand framework.

Focus area

Project or Account Representative

A representative may also be appointed for a particular customer, project, industry or product group without receiving wider territorial rights.

Every appointment will clearly define the applicable business model.
