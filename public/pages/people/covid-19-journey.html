A Message of Unity and Resilience

To Our Valued Employees, Suppliers, Customers, and Partners,

Across the world, the COVID-19 pandemic has reshaped daily life and global industry. During this period, our organization has remained guided by the same values that have defined our journey for decades—responsibility, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to the people and partners who form our global community.

At Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works, the well-being of our people remains our foremost priority, alongside the responsible continuation of manufacturing activities that support essential industries and supply chains. Through disciplined planning, strong teamwork, and shared determination, our organization continues advancing with purpose and confidence.

Prioritizing Health, Safety, and Well-Being

The well-being of our employees, suppliers, customers, and their families remains central to every decision we make. Across all our facilities, comprehensive health and safety measures operate in alignment with international public health guidance and government directives.

Enhanced workplace practices include:

  • Regular sanitization of workspaces and production areas
  • Structured social distancing practices across facilities
  • Provision of personal protective equipment (PPE) for all team members
  • Health awareness initiatives and hygiene education programs
  • Controlled facility access and organized workplace protocols

Through these initiatives, our facilities continue operating with a strong focus on employee safety, workplace discipline, and responsible manufacturing practices.

Standing Together with Our Employees

Our employees represent the foundation of our organization. Throughout this period, their dedication, professionalism, and adaptability have strengthened our ability to continue serving customers and partners across the world.

From manufacturing teams supporting production operations to colleagues coordinating operations remotely, every member of our organization demonstrates remarkable commitment and teamwork.

To support our workforce, we have introduced expanded health benefits, employee wellness initiatives, and resources that promote both professional stability and personal well-being.

Supporting Our Suppliers and Partners

Our relationships with suppliers and partners have always been built upon trust, collaboration, and long-term partnership. During this period, close coordination and open communication continue strengthening our shared ability to serve global industries.

These partnerships remain a cornerstone of our organization's growth and resilience.

Continuing to Serve Our Customers

To our valued customers across industries and regions, we express our sincere appreciation for the trust placed in our organization.

Through responsible planning and disciplined execution, our organization continues delivering the products and services that our partners depend upon.

Embracing Change and Looking Ahead

This period has strengthened our collective appreciation for resilience, adaptability, and collaboration. These experiences reinforce our commitment to sustainable growth and responsible leadership.

Gratitude and Confidence for the Future

We extend our deepest appreciation to every employee, partner, customer, and supplier who continues contributing to our shared journey.

Together, we continue advancing forward with unity, confidence, and determination.

Thank you for remaining an important part of our journey.

With appreciation and respect,

Falguni Dharmesh Trivedi

Chairwoman & Managing Director
