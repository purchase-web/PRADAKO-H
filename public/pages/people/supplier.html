Carbide Tooling & Dies

Bolt & Nut Formers

Progressive Forming

Supplier Onboarding Journey
A clear, staged path
to partnership.

Our onboarding process is designed to ensure clarity, fitment, and readiness from the outset.

1
Register

Supplier Registration

Submit company details, product or service categories, location, certifications, and capability information for initial review.

2
Submit

Document Submission

Provide process-capability details, company profile, quality systems, compliance declarations, and relevant supporting documents.

3
Review

Technical Review

Our team evaluates product fitment, technical readiness, traceability discipline, inspection capability, and document consistency.

4
Trial

Trial Order

A trial engagement validates quality, delivery reliability, and process control under real production conditions.

5
Monitor

Performance Review

Ongoing monitoring across quality, delivery, documentation, responsiveness, CAPA closure, and improvement readiness.
