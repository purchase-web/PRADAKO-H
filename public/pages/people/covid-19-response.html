Our COVID-19 Journey: A Story of Success, Commitment, and Resilience

To Our Valued Clients, Partners, and Employees,

The global pandemic created a defining period for industries across the world. During this time, our organization continued advancing forward with discipline, unity, and a deep sense of responsibility toward our employees, partners, and customers.

At Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works, this period highlighted the strength of our values and the resilience of our people. Guided by teamwork, operational discipline, and strong partnerships, our organization continued delivering excellence while supporting industries that rely on precision engineering and dependable supply chains.

This journey strengthened our relationships, enhanced our operational systems, and reinforced our commitment to responsible manufacturing and long-term collaboration.

Achieving Excellence Through Collective Commitment

Through the dedication and professionalism of our teams, our organization maintained 0 PPM (Parts Per Million) quality performance, ensuring that every fastener delivered reflected the highest manufacturing standards.

Our facilities operated with consistent stability across the entire period, supporting customers with continuous production flow and dependable delivery performance. Customer operations across industries remained fully supported through our disciplined manufacturing processes and strong operational coordination.

Demand across several sectors expanded significantly, including the healthcare industry where our fasteners supported equipment such as ventilators, ambulances, and other essential medical systems. Our teams responded with agility and dedication, ensuring that production capacity aligned with the evolving needs of our partners.

Strong operational planning, coordinated logistics, and disciplined manufacturing practices allowed our organization to maintain stable production output and consistent delivery performance throughout this period.

A Trusted Supplier During a Global Turning Point

Our customers continued placing strong confidence in our organization through their ongoing collaboration and partnership. Reliable supply, consistent quality performance, and transparent communication strengthened these relationships even further.

During this period, one valued customer elevated our role from co-supplier to sole supplier, recognizing the strength of our performance and the reliability of our manufacturing operations.

Such recognition reflects the enduring partnerships built through years of collaboration, shared commitment to quality, and a mutual focus on long-term success.

Strategic Raw Material Procurement

Early operational planning played a significant role in sustaining production stability. Our procurement teams advanced strategic material sourcing and inventory planning to support continuous manufacturing operations.

Through proactive booking, procurement coordination, and inventory management, our facilities maintained strong material availability, enabling production teams to meet growing demand while sustaining high standards of delivery reliability.

These strategic actions strengthened our supply chain stability and reinforced the resilience of our manufacturing ecosystem.

Supporting Our Suppliers and Employees

Our organization values strong partnerships across the entire ecosystem of suppliers, employees, and collaborators.

Supplier partners received consistent support through timely financial coordination and active engagement, strengthening the stability of our shared supply network.

At the same time, our employees continued their professional journey with stability, appreciation, and expanded benefits recognizing their dedication and commitment during this important period.

These initiatives reflect our enduring belief that strong organizations grow through the strength and well-being of their people and partners.

Prioritizing Health and Workplace Safety

From the earliest stages of the pandemic, comprehensive safety initiatives were implemented across our facilities to protect employees and maintain responsible operations.

Workplace practices included:

  • Provision of personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • Enhanced facility sanitization programs
  • Structured workplace hygiene initiatives
  • Organized shift planning and operational coordination

Through disciplined execution of these measures, our facilities resumed operations efficiently while maintaining a strong focus on workforce well-being and workplace responsibility.

Looking Forward with Confidence

The experiences gained during this period strengthened our operational systems, enhanced our supply chain coordination, and deepened the partnerships that support our organization's continued growth.

The dedication of our employees, the collaboration of our suppliers, and the trust of our customers together created a powerful demonstration of resilience and shared purpose.

As we continue advancing forward, these experiences serve as a foundation for stronger manufacturing capabilities, enhanced global partnerships, and sustained progress across the industries we serve.

With renewed confidence and collective determination, Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works continues its journey of engineering excellence, responsible leadership, and long-term value creation.

Thank you for your continued trust and partnership.
