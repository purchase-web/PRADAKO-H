The Distributor Journey

From application to market expansion

01

Expression of Interest

Submit your application, preferred territory, business profile and market-development proposal.

02

Preliminary Alignment

PRADAKO reviews the proposed market, product categories, industries, infrastructure and partnership model.

03

Capability Evaluation

Commercial, technical, financial, logistical and market-development capabilities are evaluated.

04

Territory & Portfolio Planning

Both teams develop the plan — target industries, customer segments, stocking strategy and market-entry approach.

05

Due Diligence & Framework

Commercial terms, responsibilities, compliance requirements, brand guidelines and territory structure are aligned.

06

Distributor Appointment

The approved distributor receives the formal partnership framework, territory definition and onboarding schedule.

07

Training & Activation

Product and standards training, technical orientation, sales enablement, portal activation and launch coordination.

08

Initial Stocking & Market Entry

The agreed product range is established and coordinated customer development begins.

09

Performance Development

Reviews across demand, sales, inventory, customer activity, market coverage and service indicators.

10

Expansion

Broader portfolios, additional territories, regional warehousing, master-distributor structures and long-term supply agreements.
