How to read this library

Three things every entry tells you

Lifecycle statusWhether the standard is current, superseded, withdrawn, or legacy — no longer current but still specified on drawings and still supplied.
Function classWhat the standard actually does: define geometry, set mechanical properties, govern a coating, prescribe a test, or control a process.
What we can makeIn current production, within our process envelope, or requiring a feasibility review first. We do not let breadth of catalogue imply breadth of capability.
Not manufacturing it today does not mean we cannot. Roughly half this library sits outside current series production. If a standard here matches your requirement, raise it as an enquiry — several of our present product lines began exactly that way.
