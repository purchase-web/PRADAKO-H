03

What the standard controls

A standard can govern material, mechanical properties, dimensions or product form without governing every other requirement. This control map keeps those roles separate.

CHECK COMPANION / ORDERProduct geometry / dimensions

Geometry and dimensional limits of the finished product.

PRIMARY / DIRECTMaterials / chemistry

Alloy/material composition and permitted material route.

PRIMARY / DIRECTMechanical properties

Strength, hardness, proof/tensile or other mechanical requirements.

CHECK COMPANION / ORDERThreads / tolerances

Thread form, pitch, class/tolerance and gauging requirements.

CHECK COMPANION / ORDERTesting / inspection

Test methods, sampling, acceptance and inspection requirements.

PRIMARY / DIRECTProduct form / condition

Bar, rod, wire, forging stock, temper, heat-treatment or delivery condition.

CHECK COMPANION / ORDERCoating / surface

Finish, plating, passivation or coating requirements.

CHECK COMPANION / ORDERQuality / documentation

Certification, marking, supplementary and customer documentation requirements.

CHECK COMPANION / ORDERProduct requirements

Product-specific material/dimensional/mechanical package.
