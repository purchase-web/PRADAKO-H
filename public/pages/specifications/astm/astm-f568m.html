01

Engineering overview

Withdrawn legacy metric carbon/alloy externally threaded fastener specification. ASTM states that it covered chemical and mechanical requirements, not dimensional requirements, and was withdrawn in 2012 with no replacement.

Before quotation, manufacture or public technical-table release

  • Lock exact ASTM edition/revision
  • Confirm product form and size range
  • Confirm grade / alloy / UNS / class
  • Confirm chemistry and product analysis
  • Confirm heat treatment / condition / temper
  • Confirm mechanical-property test route
  • Confirm threads, dimensions and tolerances
  • Confirm coating / finish where applicable
  • Confirm sampling, lot definition and acceptance
  • Confirm marking and identification
  • Confirm certification / inspection documents
  • Apply customer drawing and supplementary requirements
