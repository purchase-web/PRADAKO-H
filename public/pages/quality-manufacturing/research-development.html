Tool room

Research decides what to build. The tool room decides whether it can be built twice.

Anyone can make one good part. Making the ten-millionth identical to the first is a tooling problem — and that is where this room earns its place.

Tool Room · CNC · EDM · Grinding · Dies

Our state-of-the-art Tool Room ensures that every manufacturing process is optimized for accuracy and efficiency. With advanced CNC machines, EDM, and high-precision grinding equipment, we craft custom tools and dies that define the quality of our fasteners.

Our experienced toolmakers, working in synergy with the R&D and production teams, consistently refine processes to improve performance, reduce downtime, and enhance productivity.
