Traceability:
Ensuring Quality, Reliability and Full Lifecycle Accountability

At Pradako Mechanical and Engineering Works, traceability forms a cornerstone of our integrated quality management and manufacturing control systems, ensuring that every fastener produced within our facilities can be accurately identified, tracked, and documented throughout its entire lifecycle. Traceability enables complete visibility from raw material sourcing to final product delivery, supporting quality assurance, regulatory compliance, and customer confidence.

What our traceability system captures

Our traceability system captures and links critical manufacturing data across every stage of production, including raw material heat numbers, supplier certifications, production batch numbers, process route cards, heat treatment records, coating batches, inspection reports, and final packaging identification. Through this integrated framework, every fastener is fully traceable backward to its material origin and forward to its final shipment and application.
