CQI Compliance Systems

Commitment to Excellence: CQI-9, CQI-11 & CQI-12

At Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works we strictly adhere to AIAG CQI standards to ensure precision, durability and performance consistency across heat treatment, plating and coating processes.

CQI-9

Heat Treat System Assessment

CQI-9 establishes best practices for heat treatment processes, ensuring uniform hardness, tensile strength, and durability in metal components.

At Pradako, we maintain strict compliance through controlled furnace operations, periodic testing, and process monitoring, guaranteeing that all fasteners and precision-engineered parts meet industry and safety standards.

CQI-11

Plating System Assessment

This standard ensures high-quality plating processes, focusing on coating thickness, adhesion, and corrosion resistance.

By adhering to CQI-11, Pradako ensures that electroplated fasteners and components maintain long-term durability, enhancing performance in automotive, aerospace, and industrial applications.

CQI-12

Coating System Assessment

CQI-12 governs paint, powder coating, and other surface protection processes, ensuring defect-free, high-performance coatings.

Pradako applies strict quality controls in coating application, curing, and adhesion testing, delivering aesthetic, wear-resistant, and corrosion-resistant finishes that meet stringent industry demands.

By implementing rigorous process controls, regular self-assessments, and quality inspections, we guarantee that every part meets automotive, aerospace, and high-precision manufacturing requirements.

Our heat-treated fasteners and components exhibit superior mechanical properties, our plating processes provide exceptional corrosion resistance, and our coatings meet the highest aesthetic and functional standards.

The growing importance of these standards aligns with the increasing complexity of modern engineering components, requiring stringent quality assurance to prevent failures. Pradako's commitment to CQI compliance reinforces our position as a trusted partner, delivering high-performance solutions that meet global industry benchmarks and exceed customer expectations.
