Customer & Supplier Quality

Dedicated departments supporting one manufacturing promise.

Customer Quality

Final product confidence.

We are having Customer Quality and Supplier Quality as a separate department.

Customer Quality ensures that the final product meets or exceeds customer expectations through strict adherence to specifications, performance standards, and reliability.

Supplier Quality

Input quality discipline.

Supplier Quality focuses on maintaining consistent raw material and component quality, ensuring that suppliers meet compliance, certification, and process control requirements.

Strong customer quality has resulted into brand reputation and trust, leading to higher customer satisfaction and retention.

Robust supplier quality reduces defects, minimizes rework costs, and ensures seamless production efficiency and most importantly new vendor development and upgradation of current suppliers.
