Best Practices Followed at Pradako Mechanical and Engineering Works

1. Lean Manufacturing & Continuous Improvement

  • 5S Implementation – Organizing and maintaining an efficient, clean, and safe workspace.
  • Kanban – Just-in-time production system to reduce waste and improve efficiency.
  • Precision Engineering – Every fastener is manufactured with the highest accuracy and consistency.
  • Advanced Plating Techniques – Corrosion-resistant coatings ensure durability and longevity.
  • Kaizen – Continuous, small improvements in all processes for long-term success.
  • Suggestion and Kaizen Implementation – Encouraging employees to contribute innovative ideas for process improvements.
  • Zero Defect Policy – Strict quality control processes eliminate errors before dispatch.
  • Continuous Process Improvement – Regular evaluations to optimize production efficiency.

2. Quality Control & Error Prevention

  • Poka-Yoke Verification – Error-proofing mechanisms to eliminate defects at the source.
  • Core Tools:
  • SPC – Monitoring processes to maintain consistency.
  • MSA – Ensuring measurement accuracy and reliability.
  • PPAP – Verifying production parts meet customer requirements.
  • FMEA – Identifying and mitigating potential failures.
  • APQP – Structured approach to product and process development.
  • Control Plan – Documenting key process controls to ensure consistent quality.
  • Red Bin Analysis – Analyzing defective parts to prevent recurring issues.
  • Stringent In-House Testing – Each batch undergoes rigorous mechanical and chemical testing.
  • Traceability & Documentation – Every product is tracked from raw material to final delivery.
  • Supplier Quality Management – Only high-grade raw materials are sourced from trusted vendors.

3. Technology & Data-Driven Manufacturing

  • Advanced Process Control & Information Storage – Utilizing barcodes and digital systems.
  • Intelligent Warehousing – Automated reordering systems.
  • Innovation in Design – Customized fastening solutions.
  • Automation & Smart Manufacturing – Automated production lines.
  • Data-Driven Decision Making – AI and IoT predictive maintenance.

4. Process Optimization & Efficiency
