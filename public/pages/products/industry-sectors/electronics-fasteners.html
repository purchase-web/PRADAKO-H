Hero Product Family

SEMS Screws for Faster Electronics Assembly

SEMS combine a screw and captive washer into one controlled assembly. The washer remains retained on the screw, reducing loose-part handling, missing-washer risk, line-side part count and assembly steps — especially valuable in high-volume electronics and electrical production.

One Part · Multiple Functions

Build the SEMS Around the Joint

Start with the screw head, drive and thread. Then select the washer function, material, hardness, OD, thickness, spring behaviour, serration and final finish. The assembly can then be optimised for automatic feeding, torque control, electrical contact, anti-loosening, appearance or corrosion resistance.

  • Metric and inch screw-and-washer assemblies
  • Single or dual captive-washer combinations
  • Micro and compact SEMS for dense electronics
  • Plating thickness controlled after assembly requirements are frozen
  • Automatic sorting and washer-presence verification where specified
01 · Load Distribution

Plain Washer SEMS

Captive flat washer for bearing-area control, protection of softer surfaces and simplified line-side assembly.

02 · Anti-Loosening

Spring Washer SEMS

Machine screw with captive spring-lock washer for specified assemblies where spring action is part of the joint design.

03 · Electrical Contact

Tooth Washer SEMS

Internal or external tooth washer options for bite, anti-rotation and defined grounding/contact functions where validated.

04 · Preload

Conical / Belleville SEMS

Captive conical spring washer combinations for compact spring behaviour and preload management.

05 · Dual Function

Plain + Spring Dual SEMS

Two captive washers in one assembly to combine surface protection / load distribution with spring action.

Material + Function

Materials for Electronics Fasteners

The material is chosen around formability, strength, conductivity, corrosion resistance, magnetic response, solderability / contact requirements, galvanic compatibility, appearance and total cost.

Cold-Heading Steels

Efficient for high-speed, high-volume screws, SEMS, rivets, nuts and specials.

  • 1006 / 1008 / 1010 / 1018 / 1022-type families
  • Selected by forming severity, hardness and final mechanical requirement

Stainless Steel

For corrosion resistance, clean appearance and selected electronics environments.

  • A2 / 304-type and A4 / 316-type families
  • Passivated, natural or application-specific surface condition

Brass & Copper

Useful where conductivity, appearance, machinability or non-ferrous construction matters.

  • Brass screws, nuts, spacers and standoffs
  • Copper / copper-alloy specials for defined electrical functions

Aluminium Specials

Low-mass and appearance-sensitive parts where geometry and thread strength permit.

  • Spacers, standoffs, bushes and selected screw forms
  • Galvanic pairing must be reviewed with mating materials

Heat Treatment

Applied only when the function and material require it.

  • Case hardening for selected tapping / thread-forming screws
  • Quench & temper for higher-strength steel fasteners where applicable
  • Hydrogen de-embrittlement relief when required by material strength and coating specification

Micro Threads & Geometry

Small fasteners amplify the effect of every micron of tolerance and coating.

  • Fine head recess control, concentricity, straightness and thread start
  • Short lengths, reduced heads, small OD washers and thin sections
  • Gauging strategy defined before mass production
Colour + Thickness + Function

Plating & Finish Options for Electronics

In electronics, plating is not only about colour. The finish can affect corrosion, electrical contact, solderability, friction, assembly torque, appearance and—on micro threads—dimensional fit. Colour and coating thickness are therefore controlled as separate requirements.

Microns Matter

On M1–M3 screws and fine inch threads, an apparently small increase in deposit thickness can materially change thread fit, recess performance and assembly feel. The drawing should state the required coating system, colour, local / significant surface thickness, corrosion target and any torque or conductivity requirement.

Zero-Defect Manufacturing Philosophy

0 PPM Quality Objective

For high-volume electronics, “0 PPM” is treated as an operating objective supported by prevention, process capability and automatic detection—not as a marketing promise detached from the agreed control plan.

01

Incoming Control

Wire / bar / washer material identity, chemistry, hardness and supplier traceability as required.

02

Process Capability

SPC, control plans, MSA, capability studies and stable tooling windows for critical characteristics.

03

Poka-Yoke

Tooling and process mistake-proofing for head, recess, thread, washer assembly and secondary operations.

04

100% Sorting

Optical / dimensional / functional sorting for agreed critical parameters and washer-presence checks on SEMS.

05

Lot Release

Thread gauges, dimensions, coating thickness, appearance, count, packaging and documentation released by lot.
