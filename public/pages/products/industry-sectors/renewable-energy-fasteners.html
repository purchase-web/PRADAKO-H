RENEWABLE ENERGY FASTENERS

Solar PV · Photovoltaic Plants · Wind · Hydro

High-volume renewable-energy fasteners — from M2–M16 screws to heavy hot-forged structural and foundation fasteners.
The rapid expansion of solar and photovoltaic capacity is creating sustained demand for production-volume fastening, especially M16, M20 and M24 bolts, nuts and washers used across ground-mount structures, trackers and utility-scale plants. Pradako supports metric and inch programmes with cold forging, hot forging, thread rolling, heat treatment, zinc flake and HDG, plus project-defined coefficient-of-friction (COF) and salt-spray (SST) requirements. Supply programmes can be planned in tonnage and container-scale monthly volumes, subject to specification, tooling and production planning.

From Small Precision Parts to Heavy Forgings

Cold Forging & Hot Forging Capability

The manufacturing route is selected by diameter, upset volume, material, length, geometry, mechanical class and annual demand — not by diameter alone.

CF

Cold Forging

High-volume screws, bolts, flange fasteners, selected nuts and special headed parts.

Typical screw catalogue focus: M2 to M16. Larger cold-forged bolt / special-fastener capability is reviewed separately by heading diameter, upset ratio, length, material and geometry.

HF

Hot Forging

Large-diameter bolts, heavy hex products, long studs, anchors and turbine / foundation specials.

Typical catalogue focus: M16 to M64+* for engineered heavy fasteners; final project size is machine / tooling dependent.

TR

Thread Rolling

Rolled threads for fatigue performance, dimensional consistency and high-volume production where geometry and material permit.

Metric coarse / fine and UNC / UNF / project thread forms can be reviewed against the applicable product specification.

SP

Special Processing

Machining, bending, heading + machining combinations, washer assembly and drawing-controlled secondary operations.

Used for hanger bolts, long anchors, special shoulders, turbine studs, large foundation hardware and engineered specials.

Capability note: publish product-specific machine limits after tooling review. M16 is the published screw ceiling on this renewable-energy page. Larger bolt / special-fastener cold-forging and M64+ hot-forging capability should not be read as universally available for every head style, length, material or property class.

For zinc-containing systems, white corrosion is assessed separately from base-metal corrosion. The purchaser's specification should define the acceptance criterion.

Red rust indicates visible base-metal corrosion on carbon-steel parts. Target hours depend on coating system, thickness, topcoat, geometry and test method.
