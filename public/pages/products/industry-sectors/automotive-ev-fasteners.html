Materials + Mechanical Performance

Material & Property-Class Options

Material chemistry is selected to achieve the required forming response, hardenability, mechanical class, fatigue behaviour, corrosion compatibility and cost target.

Low / Medium Carbon Steels

Typical cold-heading choices for standard and formed fasteners.

  • SAE / AISI 1006, 1008, 1010, 1018, 1022 families
  • Selected by head geometry, forming severity and final performance

Boron & Alloy Steels

For higher strength, hardenability and demanding automotive joints.

  • 10B21, 15B25, 20MnB4 / equivalent families
  • Alloy steels such as 4140 / SCM435-type routes where specified

Stainless & Corrosion-Resistant

For corrosion, appearance, electronics and selected high-temperature environments.

  • A2 / A4 families, 304 / 316-type stainless options
  • Property class selected per applicable stainless fastener standard

Non-Ferrous Specials

For electrical, lightweight, non-sparking or appearance-sensitive applications.

  • Brass, copper and aluminium families
  • Part geometry and galvanic compatibility require application review

Heat Treatment

Route depends on fastener type, chemistry and required mechanical performance.

  • Quench & temper / through hardening for high-tensile fasteners
  • Carburising / case hardening for selected thread-forming / tapping screws
  • Stress relief / de-embrittlement bake when required by coating route and specification

Threads & Secondary Features

Functional geometry can be engineered beyond the base standard.

  • Coarse / fine metric and inch threads
  • Rolled threads, knurls, splines, cross holes, points, dog ends and collars
  • Pre-applied patch, sealant, adhesive or lubricant by customer requirement

Neutral Salt Spray / Corrosion Qualification

White Rust & Red Rust — SST Programme

Salt-spray hours must be tied to a specific coating system, thickness, topcoat, fastener geometry, condition of test and acceptance criterion. The test method alone does not create a universal hours requirement.

White Rust / White Corrosion

Typically zinc corrosion products. It indicates consumption / reaction of the sacrificial zinc-based layer and is commonly used as an appearance or coating-corrosion checkpoint where the purchaser or coating specification defines it. It is not the same endpoint as base-metal red rust.

Red Rust / Base-Metal Corrosion

Visible corrosion of the steel substrate. For zinc-flake fasteners, the no-red-rust duration is a key corrosion-performance criterion. Extended targets above standard bands require a qualified coating stack and actual part testing.
