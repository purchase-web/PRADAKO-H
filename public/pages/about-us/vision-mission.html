Vision Statement

A globally trusted, export-focused fastener group.

To build Pradako into a globally trusted, vertically integrated, export-focused fastener manufacturing group — achieving 200,000 tonnes annual manufacturing capacity by 2035, expanding international collaborations with clients and sourcing partners across strategic, technical, manufacturing, and geographic dimensions, supporting national initiatives, and becoming one of the most export-focused fastener companies globally by 2040 — while remaining guided by gratitude, responsibility, ethics, and respect for every stakeholder who contributes to our journey.

Mission Statement

To engineer trust, strength, and reliability — ahead of time.

To build a future-ready fastener manufacturing organization by strengthening our people, processes, technology, quality systems, and vertically integrated capabilities — delivering precision-engineered fasteners with complete traceability, advanced testing, sustainable operations, and customer-focused engineering support. We create long-term value for customers across the globe through quality volumes, technical excellence, confidentiality, ethics, innovation, responsible manufacturing, and strategic collaborations that support India’s growth, global exports, and sustainable industrial progress.
