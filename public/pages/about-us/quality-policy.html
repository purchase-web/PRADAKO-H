QUALITY POLICY

Engineering Excellence · Zero Defect Commitment

Commitment To Excellence

We Are Committed To Delivering Fasteners Of Uncompromising Quality, Conforming To International Standards Including EN 15048, EN 14399, EN 14592, And ISO 22163. Our Products Are Engineered For Precision, Traceability, And Reliability To Meet The Evolving Needs Of Global OEMs And Infrastructure Sectors.

Quality Management System

Our Integrated Management System (IMS), Built On ISO 9001, ISO 14001, And ISO 45001, Ensures Consistent Performance, Regulatory Compliance, And Risk-Based Thinking. We Promote A Culture Of Quality Across All Levels Of The Organization Through Clear Processes, Documentation, And Accountability.

Zero Defect Mindset

Guided By VDA 6.3 And The Zero-Defect Philosophy, We Identify, Assess, And Control Risks Across The Production Lifecycle. Our Goal Is To Prevent Recurrence, Eliminate Root Causes, And Drive Continuous Improvement Through Proactive Quality Assurance And Data-Driven Decisions.

Customer Focus

We Strive To Exceed Customer Expectations By Delivering Fasteners That Are Dimensionally Accurate, Technically Compliant, And Timely. We Treat Each Specification With Precision And Responsiveness, Ensuring Full Adherence To Client-Defined And Regulatory Requirements.

Technology & Training

We Actively Integrate Advanced Manufacturing Technologies, Digital Quality Control Systems, And Real-Time Monitoring. Our Teams Undergo Continuous Training To Remain Skilled, Agile, And Adaptive To Evolving Standards, Especially Under ISO 22163 (IRIS) And TISAX Requirements.

Supplier Collaboration

Recognizing Suppliers As Strategic Partners, We Evaluate And Collaborate With Those Who Share Our Vision Of Quality. We Engage In Joint Development, Quality Audits, And System Integration To Ensure Consistency And Performance Throughout The Value Chain.

Information Security

Aligned With ISO/IEC 27001:2022 And TISAX, We Uphold Strict Controls To Safeguard Sensitive Information, Design Confidentiality, And Customer Data. We Integrate Cyber And Operational Security As Core Pillars Of Our Digital Infrastructure And Enterprise Integrity.

Sustainability

We Operate Under ISO 50001:2018 To Optimize Energy Performance And Under ISO 14064-1:2018 & ISO 14067 To Track And Minimize Greenhouse Gas Emissions. Our Practices Are Fully Aligned With The EU's CBAM Requirements, Reflecting Our Leadership In Sustainable, Low-Carbon Manufacturing.

Ethical Conduct And Social Responsibility

We Operate With Fairness, Accountability, And Respect For Human Rights. Our Business Conduct Reflects Transparency, Ethical Sourcing, Responsible Labor Practices, And A Commitment To Environmental Stewardship.

This Policy Is Implemented Across All Functions And Reviewed Periodically To Ensure Relevance, Compliance, And Continuous Alignment With The Strategic Goals Of Pradako Mechanical And Engineering Works.
