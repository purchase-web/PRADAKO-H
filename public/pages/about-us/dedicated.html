The ELITE Program

Dedicated manufacturing plants
for strategic OEM partnerships.

The Pradako ELITE Program is a strategic manufacturing partnership model created for global OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, and large industrial organizations seeking dependable, scalable, and long-term production support.

Through dedicated manufacturing plants, exclusive production lines, integrated quality systems, and engineering collaboration, Pradako develops customer-focused manufacturing environments built around specific products, volumes, standards, and supply-chain requirements.

The difference

A supplier quotes a part. A partner builds the plant.

The ELITE Program is not a purchase order. It is a manufacturing environment configured around one customer's products, volumes and standards.

Exclusive production lines

Set up once.
Then it just runs.

Where a complete dedicated plant is not required, exclusive production lines can be configured for specific customer programs. Machines, tooling, gauges, process controls, inspection methods, and documentation are aligned with the approved product and quality requirements.

  • Consistent manufacturing conditions
  • Stable production planning
  • Controlled process parameters
  • Reduced product-mix complexity
  • Improved traceability
  • Predictable lead times

A line carrying a single approved product mix holds its process window far more tightly than a line switching between programmes.

Partnership model

Six stages. One long-term programme.

From first alignment to serial supply and future growth — a structured collaboration framework, not an open-ended arrangement.

A structured collaboration framework

How an ELITE partnership
is built.

Each stage is completed and agreed before the next begins, so that responsibility, capacity and quality expectations are clear on both sides.

01Stage

Program alignment

Product requirements, expected volumes, quality standards, delivery expectations, capacity needs, and partnership objectives are jointly defined.

02Stage

Feasibility and industrialization

Manufacturing processes, materials, tooling, inspection methods, testing requirements, and packaging standards are evaluated and developed.

03Stage

Manufacturing setup

The required production environment, machinery, tooling, quality systems, material flow, and operational resources are configured.

04Stage

Quality integration

Traceability systems, control plans, testing arrangements, documentation, and customer-specific compliance requirements are implemented.

05Stage

Validation and production launch

Trial production, product validation, process approval, production-readiness reviews, and controlled ramp-up are completed.

06Stage

Long-term partnership

Ongoing collaboration supports supply stability, continuous improvement, cost optimization, new-product development, and future capacity growth.
