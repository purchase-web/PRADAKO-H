Why public markets

Growth pursued
with discipline.

WHY

Pradako has been built through manufacturing experience, customer trust, engineering knowledge, and a commitment to dependable execution.

As we prepare for the organisation’s next phase, we are evaluating how access to public markets could support responsible expansion, strengthen institutional capabilities, and create a broader platform for long-term value creation.

A proposed IPO would not be pursued merely for scale. It would be considered as part of a carefully structured strategy to strengthen the organisation, deepen its manufacturing capabilities, support market expansion, and establish a stronger foundation for future growth.

Our objective is to grow without compromising the principles that have shaped Pradako: product integrity, process discipline, responsible commitments, and respect for every stakeholder associated with the organisation.

We are therefore working toward the governance, reporting, operational, compliance, and organisational standards expected of a public-market company.

Any proposed offering will remain subject to internal approvals, regulatory requirements, market conditions, professional advice, and other applicable considerations.

We are not pursuing scale for its own sake. We are preparing to build a stronger, more accountable, and more enduring institution.

Our growth priorities

Capital directed
with purpose.

Should an Initial Public Offering be undertaken, the deployment of capital would be guided by disciplined planning, responsible governance, and the long-term interests of the organisation and its stakeholders.

The final utilisation of any proceeds would be described in the relevant offer documents. Our present areas of focus include the following.

Manufacturing Capacity and Capability

Strengthening production capabilities to support evolving customer programmes, increasingly complex components, and long-term supply requirements.

The emphasis would remain on sustainable capacity development rather than expansion without operational justification.

Technology and Modernisation

Advancing manufacturing technology, quality infrastructure, automation, process monitoring, traceability, inspection systems, and operational intelligence.

These investments would support greater consistency, reliability, productivity, and manufacturing control.

Product and Application Engineering

Expanding engineering capabilities to support application-specific requirements, customised fastener solutions, development programmes, and technically demanding industries.

Global Market Development

Building stronger international customer relationships, distribution channels, local partnerships, market-development capabilities, and responsive supply systems.

Our objective is to move closer to the customers and industries we serve while retaining the strength of our manufacturing base.

People and Leadership Development

Developing the technical, managerial, financial, compliance, and leadership capabilities required for a larger and more institutionally governed organisation.

Governance and Enterprise Systems

Strengthening financial controls, reporting frameworks, risk-management systems, compliance processes, data governance, internal accountability, and organisational transparency.

Long-Term Business Resilience

Supporting the organisation’s ability to respond to changing customer requirements, supply-chain developments, market conditions, regulatory expectations, and emerging industrial opportunities.
