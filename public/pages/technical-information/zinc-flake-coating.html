The Pradako approach
02 / 10

Sacrificial protection,
by design.

At Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works, zinc flake coating is approached as an advanced non-electrolytic system engineered for superior corrosion resistance, controlled friction, and long-term durability.

These are sacrificial systems: the zinc and aluminium elements oxidise preferentially, ensuring the underlying steel substrate stays protected. They're developed for automotive and industrial components needing higher protection than conventional electroplating or paint.

Particularly suited where hydrogen-embrittlement risk must be eliminated — ideal for high-tensile fasteners and safety-critical assemblies.

Application scope

Built for fasteners

Zinc flake coating is primarily applied on fasteners — particularly suited to non-flexing or minimally-flexing components and applications that can withstand elevated curing temperatures.

It also guards against galvanic corrosion where dissimilar metals meet — highly beneficial in modern lightweight engineering involving aluminium components.

Process overview

Non-electrolytic coating technology.

04 / 10

Controlled application of zinc-flake dispersion onto steel — typically with aluminium flakes — within a defined medium. Controlled curing bonds the flakes to each other and to the substrate, forming an inorganic, electrically conductive layer that delivers cathodic protection.

  • ✓Non-electrolytic process — eliminates hydrogen-embrittlement risk
  • ✓Uniform coating thickness across complex geometries
  • ✓Multi-layer systems — basecoat with optional topcoat
  • ✓Controlled curing cycles for consistent performance
  • ✓Electrically conductive layer ensures cathodic protection
