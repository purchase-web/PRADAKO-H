Engineering Overview

A functional coating system,
not only a finish.

PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene) is a high-performance fluoropolymer coating system used in fasteners to deliver controlled friction, chemical resistance, and long-term operational stability.

It is applied as part of an engineered coating system, typically consisting of a base layer for corrosion protection and a PTFE-based topcoat for lubrication and friction control. PTFE functions as a dry film lubricant, enabling smooth assembly, precision-controlled torque behavior, and consistent performance across demanding applications.
