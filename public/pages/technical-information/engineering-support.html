Engineering Support

What is an Engineering Support Team in fasteners?

An Engineering Support Team in fasteners is a dedicated technical team that helps customers select, design, validate, and optimize fasteners for specific applications. The team works as a bridge between customer engineering, purchase, quality, and production requirements and the manufacturer’s capabilities in material, tooling, forging, threading, heat treatment, coating, inspection, and documentation.

Why it matters

Fasteners may look small, but they directly influence joint strength, assembly safety, corrosion resistance, product life, and final equipment reliability. Engineering support helps avoid wrong fastener selection, unclear specifications, manufacturing delays, rework, field failures, and unnecessary cost.

  • Helps customers select the correct fastener type, material, grade, standard, coating, and inspection requirement.
  • Reviews drawings and technical specifications before production to reduce errors and quality risks.
  • Connects application conditions with manufacturing feasibility, testing requirements, and long-term product performance.
  • Supports customers in solving issues related to torque failure, thread stripping, corrosion, coating failure, breakage, and assembly mismatch.
