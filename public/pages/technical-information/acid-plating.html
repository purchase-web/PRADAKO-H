Zinc Plating · Sacrificial Protection

Corrosion protection through
controlled zinc deposition.

02 / 08

Zinc plating is one of the most widely used coating processes for fasteners, offering effective corrosion protection through sacrificial action. In addition to acting as a sacrificial layer, zinc forms a stable oxide layer on the surface, which unlike iron oxide, does not degrade the substrate but instead provides an additional barrier against further oxidation, enhancing long-term corrosion resistance.

At Pradako Mechanical & Engineering Works, zinc plating is carried out with precise control over coating thickness, uniformity, and passivation, ensuring consistent protection across all surfaces. We offer a range of finishes including clear, blue, yellow, and black passivation, tailored to specific application requirements.

Zinc plating is executed through a multi-stage electrochemical process, where each stage is carefully controlled to ensure coating integrity and adhesion. The process typically includes:

The electroplating process involves the fastener acting as the cathode, where zinc ions are deposited onto the surface under controlled electrical current, ensuring uniform coating formation even across complex geometries.

Application-Driven Process Selection

Selection of plating systems is based on component geometry, performance requirements, and end-use environment. Acid zinc plating is typically preferred for applications requiring high deposition efficiency, bright finish, and cost-effective coating solutions. However, for components with complex geometries or where uniform thickness distribution is critical, alternative plating systems may be considered.

Performance-Oriented Coating Control

Coating performance is defined not only by process control, but by measurable output parameters. Coating thickness is maintained within controlled micron ranges, ensuring uniform protection without affecting thread functionality or assembly performance. Corrosion resistance is validated through salt spray testing, with coatings engineered to perform across defined exposure durations based on customer and application requirements.
