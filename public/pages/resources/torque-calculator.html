Read before using these numbers

Output is indicative engineering guidance for a standard, well-lubricated, elastic joint. It does not account for gasket creep, embedding, thermal cycling, eccentric loading, thread engagement length, soft or coated mating materials, or reused fasteners. Friction coefficient is the single largest source of error — a µ wrong by 0.02 shifts torque by roughly 10%. Always verify against your joint design, the applicable standard and your own procedure before tightening anything.
