Bolt head marking identifier

You are holding a bolt and you do not know what it is. Match the marks on the head to the grade, strength, material and standard — metric ISO 898, SAE inch, ASTM structural, stainless and nuts.

Markings identify a grade — they do not certify one

Head marks are applied by the manufacturer and are routinely counterfeited on low-cost imports. A mark alone is not evidence of conformity. For any load-bearing or safety-critical application, require a mill test certificate traceable to the batch, and test if the application warrants it. Unmarked bolts should be treated as the lowest grade in the family until proven otherwise.
