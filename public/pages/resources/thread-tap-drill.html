Thread pitch & tap drill

Metric coarse, metric fine and UN inch series. Tap drill sizes for 65%, 75% and 100% thread engagement, plus ISO 273 clearance holes in fine, medium and coarse fit.

The metric shop standard d − P is about 92% engagement — not 75%, despite what many charts claim. The 75% figure is an inch-practice convention. Going from 75% to 100% roughly doubles tapping torque and tap breakage for only a few percent more strength; it is almost never worth it in production.

Ductile materials cut oversize

In aluminium, copper and mild steel the tap displaces material and the finished hole runs undersize on thread. Drill trials on the actual material before committing a production tapping operation.
