Container & pallet load calculator

Pieces to cartons to pallets to containers — and, more usefully, which constraint is actually limiting the load. For cartonised fasteners at normal load heights it is almost always floor space, not payload: a single-tier 20 ft of 1100 mm pallets typically uses under a third of the permitted weight. The lever is stacking height and double-tiering, not lighter packing.

Defaults assume M8 × 40 hex in 25 kg cartons on a Euro pallet, 1100 mm load height. Carton tare adds packaging weight on top of net product.

Planning figure, not a stowage plan

Assumes a single carton size stacked in a regular block pattern, even weight distribution and no mixed-SKU consolidation. Real loading is affected by pallet overhang rules, stacking strength of the carton, IMO/road axle limits at destination, and whether the port applies a lower VGM cap. Confirm with your freight forwarder before booking.
