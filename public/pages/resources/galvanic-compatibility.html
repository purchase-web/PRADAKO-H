Galvanic compatibility matrix

Put a stainless bolt into an aluminium bracket near salt water and the bracket dissolves, not the bolt. This checks fastener against joint material using anodic index difference — and flags the area ratio trap that causes most real failures.

The area ratio matters more than most people think

Corrosion concentrates on the anodic (less noble) member. A small anodic area against a large cathodic one corrodes fast and deep. A stainless fastener in an aluminium panel is the classic bad case — small noble bolt, large active panel, corrosion spread thinly is tolerable. Reverse it, an aluminium rivet in a large stainless panel, and the rivet is eaten in months. Design so the fastener is the more noble member wherever you can.
