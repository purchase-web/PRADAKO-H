Hardness conversion is indicative only

Hardness scales measure different things and no exact conversion exists. Values follow ASTM A370 / ISO 18265 for non-austenitic steels and are invalid for stainless, non-ferrous, case-hardened or heavily cold-worked material. Never use a converted value for acceptance — test on the scale the specification calls for.
